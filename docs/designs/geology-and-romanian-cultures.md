## Problem Statement

Add geological maps and maps of archaeological cultures as ordinary layers, each with a named source. No new map types or modes: "i just need layers as before, no fancy options for now. just layers and sources for them".

> After the build (2026-11-24, asked by the user): units that straddle the end of the Precambrian, 1.29% of the land at zoom 2, get two classes, "Neoproterozoic to Paleozoic" and "Precambrian to Paleozoic", instead of staying clear as in D8.

> Corrected after the build: RAN's table must be read without quoting, as stray quotation marks joined nine rows. The figures become 16,138 sites, 6,584 with a culture, 2,940 of those with coordinates, 98 over 10 km from their commune (the farthest 386 km), Botoșani 681 of 681, and a commune point off by 3.2 km in the median (90% within 6.8 km). Cultures are named in one option with commas between them, and drawn at most eight at once.

> Names (2026-11-24, decided by the user): 29 cultures' other spellings and slips are joined (`JOINED` in `ro_cultures.py`), and nine entries that are no culture are dropped. That leaves 6,580 sites with a culture. Names that may be a phase or a regional group (Cucuteni B, Cernavodă I to III, Sighișoara - Wietenberg, the Dacian names and so on) stay split until the user has researched them.

## Constraints

- A wrong name or place is worse than a missing one: no invented culture boundaries.
- Same shape as the existing layers: one module each, one-time cached download, key, credit line, recipe, web form, atlas, README section, tests.
- No new dependencies.

## Decisions

- **Geology is a world map first** (the user's message during the eng review); Romania is one place to check it.
- **Geology comes from Macrostrat's vector tiles** (`tiles.macrostrat.org/carto/{z}/{x}/{y}.mvt`, D2, not `carto-slim`), worldwide, CC BY 4.0. Ages come from `b_int_id` and `t_int_id` through Macrostrat's interval table, cached once.
- **Globes use zoom 2** (D7): one map, the Generalized Geology of the World (Chorlton 2007, source 154), on 97% of land. Closer maps use zooms 3 to 5 by size and mix regional maps (over Romania source 147, 1:5M).
- **Geology credits name each original map on the page.** Every unit has a `source_id`; `macrostrat.org/api/v2/defs/sources` gives its authors.
- **Cultures come from RAN**, the national register, 2017 CSV on data.gov.ro (OGL-ROU-1.0). The newer 2025 file has coordinates for only 949 of 27,502 records and no culture field.
- **Cultures are drawn as sites, never as areas.** The open outlines found were wrong or unsourced.
- **Sites without coordinates are placed at their commune with one hollow mark per culture and commune, sized by count** (1, 2-4, 5 or more; D10). Cultures that share a commune are set side by side, so none hides another. Drawing only located sites would mislead: Botoșani has 681 of 682 culture sites located, Iași 10 of 153, Vrancea 0 of 59.
- **A located site more than 10 km from its stated commune counts as unlocated.** 94 of 2,939 do; one is 412 km off.
- **XRONOS stays out.** It fails the "if usable" test for Romania (below).
- **Front lines wait.** No open dataset was found; they would have to be traced from old maps.

## Approaches Considered

### Approach A: geology and Romanian cultures (chosen)
Two modules, `geology.py` and `ro_cultures.py`, on sources already tested.

### Ruled out
- All three layers with traced fronts: deferred by the user.
- Ready-made culture outlines: OpenHistoricalMap starts Cucuteni near 5800 BC and cites no source; historical-basemaps (GPL-3.0) lacks Cucuteni.
- IGME5000 shapefile: the same map as Macrostrat's source 147, Europe only, and its zip carries stricter general terms than its catalogue record.

## Recommended Approach

### `--geology [age|rock]`
- `age` (default): the smallest epoch (Cenozoic only), period, era or eon containing the unit's whole age (D5, D8); before the Cambrian, eras, eons and one Precambrian class (D9). Units with none, undated units and ice stay clear. Standard time scale colours from Macrostrat's table.
- `rock`: sedimentary, volcanic, plutonic, other igneous or metamorphic only where a unit has one class; Mixed for several; clear for none (D4). The tile lists classes alphabetically, so the first is not the main rock.
- `--geology-alpha`, as the other overlays have.
- Tile zoom: 2 for a globe or hemisphere; for closer maps the largest of 3 to 5 that needs at most 64 tiles (Romania 5, Europe 4). Tiles are cached under `~/.cache/ortho_tiles/geology/`; a failed tile is never cached and is reported through `incomplete.note`. Tiles end at 85°, so the South Pole has none.
- Grid: global 0.05° for globes (D6). Closer maps use a window from `climate.visible_box`, whose longitudes are relative to the map's centre, so 180° needs no special case; its cell follows the output's pixels, capped by `MAX_CELLS` as in `climate.py`, never finer than 0.01° (D12). The unchanged `biomes.burn` fills it with shifted points. `gbif._fields` and `_tag_pairs` are reusable; `gbif._number` is not (0 for decimals and text). Units of geometry type 2 are skipped. `biomes._key` draws the key, from the classes at `biomes.visible_points`, so it lists only what the map shows.
- Colours land only, through `climate.land_mask`, which also takes the lakes out.

### `--ro-cultures NAME [NAME ...]`
- One colour per culture; filled mark where located, hollow where placed at the commune; the key counts both.
- Names match ignoring case, hyphens and diacritics. Real mergers ("Cucuteni - Ariușd" into Cucuteni) are not made automatically. An unknown name lists the close ones.
- No dates or periods are added to a culture name.
- Reuse: `ro_admin` for commune outlines. `ro_admin.load_admin` drops the SIRUTA code, so `ro_admin` gains one function that returns communes by code, and `load_admin` stays as it is. The locality-to-commune link is SIRUTA's `SIRSUP`, read from `SIRUTA_URL`, cached.
- Map text in Romanian follows the existing `--language` rules, with comma-below ș and ț.

## Known limits (said in the README and the credits)

- The European map shows the rocks under the Quaternary cover, so most loess and river deposits do not appear. At 1:5M it suits a country or larger, not a valley.
- RAN is the 2017 state and reflects where archaeologists surveyed. An empty county is not an empty past.

## Open Questions

- Should the school atlas get a geology page and a cultures page? The layers do not depend on it.
- Macrostrat publishes its tiles as CC BY 4.0, while BGR's own zip includes stricter general terms. Printing maps with credit looks fine; ask BGR if the tiles' data is ever redistributed.

## Success Criteria

- A world geology map shows the Canadian, Baltic and African shields as Precambrian, Siberia's Triassic traps and no seams; Australia's interior reads Cenozoic, not Paleocene.
- A Romania geology map shows the Miocene of the Transylvanian Basin apart from the Carpathians.
- A Cucuteni map shows Iași, Vrancea and Bacău sites, with hollow marks where placed by commune.
- Every culture named in the key appears in RAN under that name.
- Credits name every source map used. The full test suite passes.
